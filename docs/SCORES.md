## Payback check

The operator says it recovers costs in 2 to 3 years. At the median P50 value per block with ConnectedSolutions, $38,430 a year, a simple payback of 2 years implies an installed cost of $76,859 per block and 3 years $115,289, against the owner's estimates of $180,000 and $260,000. This is a consistency check on the cost estimates, not evidence for or against the claim: simple payback ignores financing, O&M and the host's share.
