# Validation

Generated by `make validate` on 2026-10-21 from the local database. It tests what public data can test and says what it cannot. Everything about revenue is simulated.

## Summary

- Annual energy, towns held out: on 700 pilot buildings the model ranks with Spearman 0.47 against 0.43 for size-only, and its median error is 70% against 77% (statewide method check, 8,462 buildings: 0.50 against 0.43).
- Where the model is closer than size-only (segments of 30 or more buildings): generic, warehouse. Where it is not: manufacturing. Within one segment the two rank buildings alike.
- Calibration: the 80 percent energy band holds the measured value 79% of the time, so it is calibrated overall.
- Rank stability: sites in the top 15 in more than half the draws, Metro South 12, Worcester 0. Worcester's ranking is a tier, not an order.
- Peak shape is unvalidated, only stress-tested: with the reduction share cut, Metro South keeps 14 of its top 15 and Worcester 8.
- EnerNOC industrial sites (25) often fall outside the model's range: summer 3 to 8 pm mean over peak (only 68% inside, median 0.57 against 0.14 to 0.69). Config is not changed.
- EnerNOC school sites (25) often fall outside the model's range: peak to average (only 32% inside, median 3.46 against 3.78 to 8.24). Config is not changed.
- With assumed peaks of 100 / 350 / 750 / 1,574 kW, EnerNOC light industrial sites cut a median 0.29 / 0.21 / 0.16 / 0.12 of their monthly peak with one block; see the rescale table for where that sits in the no-profile range the model draws from. This tests shape at an assumed peak, not real kW.
- Hospital sanity check, not accuracy: of 24 scored hospitals, none fail (load factor inside the 9 ComStock Massachusetts hospitals' range and peak at or above average in every draw); 4 sit outside their kWh or peak per sq ft range (tier 1).
- Value per block has no accuracy number. It is simulated, gross, before the host share, and unvalidated.

## Annual energy against LBER, towns held out

Each LBER building with measured grid electricity (zero or negative is missing) is estimated as if unmeasured: floor area times its segment's median kWh per sq ft, with the priors recomputed from every other town by the Phase 3 rules. Size-only is floor area times one global median from the same other towns. Spearman is the rank correlation with measured kWh (higher is better); the error is the median absolute percent error (lower is better). LBER covers only buildings of about 20,000 sq ft and up, so this says nothing about smaller buildings.

Within one segment both estimates are floor area times a constant, so they rank buildings the same way; the model can only rank better across segments, and it can be closer in level. Spearman needs three or more buildings (n/a below that). Errors this large partly reflect the first LBER release, where many reported values are implausibly low (config/intensity.yaml).

## Hospital sanity check

A sanity check, not validation and not accuracy. Each scored hospital's modeled yearly electricity, implied average kW (yearly kWh over 8,760 hours), modeled monthly peak kW and monthly load factor (average over peak), each the median of the model's own draws, next to the 9 Massachusetts hospitals in the ComStock sample (2018 weather).

- Load factor: circular. The model draws hospital load factors from these same ComStock hospitals, so this only checks that no draw left their range.
- Peak and average: the model divides yearly kWh by the load factor, capped at 1, so a peak below the implied average should never happen; the check confirms it in every draw.
- Tier 1 yearly kWh: circular. A measured building's kWh is LBER's reported electricity, an input to the model, so the LBER column is shown for reference, not as validation.
- kWh and peak per sq ft: independent of ComStock, since the hospital kWh prior comes from LBER pilot hospitals (config/intensity.yaml), but against only 9 simulated buildings, so outside their range is a flag to look at, not a failure.

## What is unvalidated

- Peak shape: the monthly peak reduction share, load factor and event-window load all come from ComStock simulation (one weather year, 2018, perfect foresight reduced by a placeholder realization factor). Stress-tested, its unit-free measures checked against EnerNOC meters, and the share of a block's cut checked on EnerNOC shapes at assumed peaks only, since the dataset hides real kW.
- ConnectedSolutions delivery: the rate and terms are sourced from the program documents, but what a block actually delivers in events (the performance factor, the overlap with the building's own peaks, and whether the site exports) is a placeholder.
- Cost and profit are not modeled. Value per block is gross revenue; the reader applies their own installed cost and host share.
- The Eversource rate area for Canton and Sharon is unconfirmed: the rate is the Greater Boston one, and no Eversource document lists towns by area.
- Factories, laundries, food plants, cold storage and auto repair have no open load profile: they take the widest simulated range in their size bin.
- Matching LBER rows to buildings is reported in docs/MATCH_RATES.md, but ownership grouping and keyword segments were only hand-checked on a small, non-random sample.

## Limits

Value per block is a simulated, gross yearly revenue per battery block, before any host share. It is not profit and not accurate, and no measurement here supports an accuracy figure for it. What is tested is the annual energy estimate against LBER, which covers only buildings of about 20,000 sq ft and up and whose first release has many implausibly low values, the stability of the ranking under the model's own uncertainty, and its sensitivity to two data rules. A site's place in the list is a reason to talk to it, not a forecast of its savings.
