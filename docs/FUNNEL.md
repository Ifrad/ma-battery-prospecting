# Funnel

Generated by `make funnel` from the database and config. Rerun it after `make sites`.

Candidate sites that survive each filter, in order. A site dropped by a filter stays in the `site_funnel` table with the filter that dropped it; nothing is deleted. Survivors still carry unvalidated peak shape: their peak is estimated from yearly kWh and a simulated load factor, not measured.

1. The utility runs ConnectedSolutions (config/connected_solutions.yaml). 448 sites served by municipal light plants are dropped here, flagged municipal_plant; 330 of them are in the five towns whose plants publish no rate schedule (config/rates.yaml).
2. Estimated use above 10,000 kWh a month, the National Grid G-1 line: LBER measured kWh where a building has it, otherwise floor area times the segment's median intensity (config/intensity.yaml).
3. Estimated peak at least 200 kW: average kW from yearly kWh, divided by the median monthly load factor of the site's simulated type (config/simulation.yaml). Sites with no open profile or an unmapped use code take the pooled median and are flagged.

Thresholds are placeholders in config/funnel.yaml.
